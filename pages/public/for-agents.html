Questions from agents

Is it really free to join?

Yes. Creating your profile and getting on a lake page costs nothing — no card required. Paid plans exist for featured placement and area priority, but you never have to upgrade to be listed and matched.

Do I have to leave my brokerage?

No. We're a directory and matchmaking service, not a brokerage. You stay exactly where you are, keep your license and your team, and keep working the way you already do.

Do you take a commission or referral fee?

Never. We don't touch your deals. Every dollar you close is yours — there's no split and no referral cut on anything that comes through the site.

How do leads work — will I get spammed?

The opposite. Buyers and sellers tell us the lake they care about and what they're after, and we route them to the right local agent. You get warm, relevant introductions — not a firehose of junk.

How many agents are on each lake?

We keep it focused so the local expert stands out — a lake page isn't a crowded portal wall of a hundred names. Upgrading to a paid plan adds featured placement and priority in your areas.

What does a paid plan add?

Featured placement on your lakes and towns, priority in matching, and a more prominent profile. You'll see the plan options right after you create your profile — pick free, or level up when the leads are worth it.

How do I get started?

Click any "Create your free profile" button on this page. You'll fill in your details, choose your lakes, and pick a plan — free or paid — at the end. That's it.
